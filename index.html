How it works

From a voice note to a working fix.

  1. Report

    Speak, type or send a photo in your own language. An AI agent asks what is missing and drafts a brief.

  2. Verify

    A local NSS, UBA or NGO volunteer confirms it is real. Dangerous issues go straight to the authority.

  3. Ideathon

    A faculty mentor is matched. Student teams compete to design the best fix.

  4. Fix

    Sponsors fund the top idea. You confirm it works.
